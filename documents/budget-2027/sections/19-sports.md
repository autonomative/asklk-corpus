# 19. Sports

<!-- p. 43 -->

We are focusing on building the common infrastructure required for the training of sportsmen and sportswomen to win victories at national and international level, and for building a sports culture among the general public for a healthy life, as well as on building human resources comprising skilled referees and coaches.

• A National High-Performance Centre will be established to provide sports science methods, physical fitness training, nutrition counselling, sports

<!-- p. 44 -->

medicine services and mental preparation facilities to elevate the talents of sportsmen and sportswomen to international level. Its first phase will commence at the Torrington Stadium, and Rs. 300 million has been allocated for establishing the second phase within the Diyagama Sports Complex.

• Provisions of Rs. 1,500 million have been allocated for the development and modernization of the Sugathadasa National Indoor Sports Complex, the purchase of the required sports equipment and the construction of a synthetic running track at the Sugathadasa International Stadium.

• Rs. 2,500 million has been allocated to establish a sports culture with the aim of enhancing the sports skills of school students and the youth community and directing the elderly generation towards sports. • It is proposed to increase the Rs. 50 million currently allocated for construction work, including the 400-metre synthetic running track at the Anuradhapura Sports Complex, up to Rs. 1,000 million, in order to increase access to international-standard sports facilities for the North Central, Northern and Eastern Provinces.

• The public and private sectors will be encouraged to increase access to private and State universities and to provide employment opportunities to secure the future of school children who demonstrate high sporting talent at national level. • As appreciation awards for gold medalists at international competitions, it is proposed to provide Rs. 100 million for an Olympic gold medal, Rs. 100 million for a World Athletics Championships gold medal, Rs. 75 million for a Commonwealth gold medal, Rs. 50 million for an Asian gold medal and Rs. 15 million for a SAARC gold medal. Correspondingly, it is also proposed to provide appreciation awards for silver and bronze medals at these competitions and for other international competitions.

• Rs. 77.5 million has been allocated for the year 2027 to establish a Youth Political Leadership Academy at an estimated cost of Rs. 208 million, with the aim of training young men and women for political and community leadership and involving them in regional and national level decision-making processes.

Further, Rs. 45 million has been allocated for research and development activities to encourage innovation in the youth sector.

<!-- p. 45 -->

Including these proposals, an investment of Rs. 8,500 million has been allocated for development activities in the youth affairs and sports sector for the year 2027.
