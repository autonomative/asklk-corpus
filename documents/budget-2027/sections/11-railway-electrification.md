# 11. Railway Electrification

<!-- p. 28 -->

• The development of the railway sector, which is considered as the backbone of passenger transport, has been identified as a Government’s priority. Accordingly, an investment of over Rs. 1,200 billion is scheduled to be made to electrify three main railway lines. For the implementation of the first phase of this programme, for the year 2027;

o Rs. 45,000 million for the electrification of the Kelani Valley railway line from Maradana to Homagama; o Rs. 16,400 million for the electrification of the Main line from Maradana to Ragama; o Rs. 17,700 million for the electrification of the Coastal line from Maradana to Panadura;

o Rs. 900 million for other operational expenses, including land acquisition, it is proposed to allocate Rs. 80,000 million, including the above.
