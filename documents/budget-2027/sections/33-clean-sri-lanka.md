# 33. Clean Sri Lanka

<!-- p. 65 -->

• The 'Clean Sri Lanka' programme aims to transform Sri Lankan life, extending well beyond mere physical cleanliness, by steering it toward a positive direction in social, environmental and ethical terms. Under its social pillar, the programme seeks to strengthen quality of life, dignity, safety, equal access, inclusivity, community participation and social harmony; under its environmental pillar, it focuses on promoting environmental protection, conservation, stewardship, biodiversity, responsible resource utilization and sustainability; and under its ethical pillar, it seeks to strengthen responsibility, honesty, discipline, fairness, efficiency and accountability regarding public resources. Rs. 6,000 million has been allocated to implement these programmes.

• Various programs are being implemented, including: the provision and sustainable maintenance of physical infrastructure, such as Haritha Mawatha,' 'Clean Colombo,' 'Clean Islands,' 'Clean City,' 'Clean Sri Lanka-Aththama,' the modernization of 'Dream Destination' railway stations, and the establishment of standardized public sanitation facilities; environmental development projects, including the creation of elephant nourishment zones; and initiatives to promote positive public habits, community participation, and corporate responsibility.

• Under the "Pivithuru Purawara" (Clean Cities) National Waste Management Program, the objective is to increase the waste collection rate, currently at 32 percent to 60 percent by 2029, while promoting safe waste management and fostering a healthy, secure environment. It is proposed to allocate Rs. 5,000
