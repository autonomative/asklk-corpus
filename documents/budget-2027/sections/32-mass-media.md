# 32. Mass Media

<!-- p. 64 -->

• Provisions have been allocated, amounting to Rs. 400 million to the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation and Rs. 489 million to the Independent Television

<!-- p. 65 -->

Network, for implementing digitalization activities, improving broadcasting facilities and purchasing modern equipment and technological tools. Rs. 384 million has been allocated to the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation for recurrent expenditure. • It is proposed to allocate Rs. 100 million to offer higher education scholarships and to provide support for the technological access required in journalism, in order to provide the necessary support for the skills development of journalists in keeping with the advancement in the field of mass media in parallel with modern technology.
