# 29. Judiciary

<!-- p. 62 -->

The most fundamental basis for the realization of democracy and the Rule of Law in a country is the ability to administer justice fairly and without delay. The judicial and prison sector has come under severe pressure due to the long-standing shortage of infrastructure, technological underdevelopment and systemic delays.

It is proposed to provide solutions by upgrading the infrastructure of the judicial sector, integrating modern technology and carrying out systemic reforms. • Rs. 5,115 million has been allocated for the expansion and development of facilities in the existing courts in Jaffna, Batticaloa, Trincomalee, Mannar, Nuwara Eliya, Kegalle, Gampaha, Kalutara, Kandy and Colombo.

• Rs. 60 million has been allocated for the complete digitalization of the activities of the Government Analyst's Department. • The Supreme Court e-filing portal, the capacity for paperless hearings, integrated case management and online payments (e-CMS) have already been implemented as components of the programme to obtain the support of digitalization for the efficiency of the judicial system. The implementation of this program within the Supreme Court has now been completed, and the process for its implementation in other courts has commenced.

Severe congestion has arisen within the prison system of our country. Exceeding the capacity to hold around 16,500 persons in 22 prisons and 14 work camps, nearly 36,500 inmates, around twice that number, are currently being held. Officers as well as inmates have lost their lives due to unfortunate incidents that occurred within prisons as a result of this congestion and the drug menace.

• Due to the severe congestion in the Colombo prison complex, including the Welikada Prison, the Magazine Prison and the Colombo Remand Prison, it is proposed to allocate a sum of Rs. 300 million in this Budget for the commencement of the relocation of these prison complexes to the Millewa area.

• In line with the Galle Heritage City Development Plan and tourism zone promotion activities, it is proposed to allocate a sum of Rs. 500 million in this Budget for the commencement of the relocation of the current Galle Prison from its location to the Boossa area.

<!-- p. 63 -->

• Rs. 1,000 million has been allocated for the expansion of the Wariyapola and Watareka Prisons, where development work has already commenced, to increase their holding capacity. • Rs. 500 million has been allocated for the relocation of the Matara Prison to the Kotawilawatta area in Matara.
