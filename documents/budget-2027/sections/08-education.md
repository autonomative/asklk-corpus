# 8. Education

<!-- p. 22 -->

Education holds the greatest responsibility in building the human resources required for the modern world. Therefore, a qualitative transformation in education as well as a new technological transformation in the delivery of education is required. For this, it is essential to develop the necessary infrastructure and human resources.

• The new education reforms have already commenced for Grade 1 and will commence for Grades 2 and 6 next year. • To ease the financial burden on trainees undergoing teacher training at the Colleges of Education, it is proposed to increase the monthly allowance paid to them from Rs. 10,500 to Rs. 20,000 with effect from April 2027. This is expected to serve as financial support during their teacher training period, as well as to help prepare them to assume the future responsibilities of our education system.

• To support students pursuing their education at Pirivenas and to help meet their educational needs, we are taking steps to increase the daily allowance provided to student monks from Rs. 15 to Rs. 50, and the daily allowance for lay students from Rs. 5 to Rs. 25, with effect from April 2027.

• With the aim of producing a generation of children endowed with values, morality and virtue, steps will be taken to increase by Rs. 5,000, from Rs. 7,500 to Rs. 12,500, the annual allowance currently paid from 2027 to Dhamma School teachers who contribute voluntarily to safeguard the quality of Dhamma School education.

<!-- p. 23 -->

• Steps will be taken to provide the school uniforms donated by the Government of China in the same manner next year as well. The Government of China has expressed its agreement to provide this donation for the next few years as well.

## 8.1 Digital Transformation Programme in School Education

• Our target is to provide reliable internet connections to all secondary schools. At present, reliable internet connections have been provided to 6,225 schools, including 3,009 connections provided this year. Steps will be taken to provide connections to the remaining 750 schools expeditiously. This programme will also be launched for primary schools.

• Providing equipment, including computers and smart boards, required for digital teaching, developing Information Technology and distance learning centres, improving the National Education Management Information System (NEMIS), providing accessible, interactive digital learning facilities for students with disabilities, and enhancing the digital literacy and technical skills of teachers are included in this 5-year programme. An estimate of Rs. 96,500 million has been made for these programmes, and it is proposed to allocate a further Rs. 3,000 million in addition to the Rs. 4,000 million allocated this year for its commencement.

• Accordingly, Rs. 35,000 million has been allocated in 2027 for digitalization, the implementation of teacher capacity development programmes, and the development of other infrastructure, including the development and modernization of Teachers' Colleges, National Colleges of Education, school buildings, school laboratories and teachers' hostels.

## 8.2 Higher Education

• Rs. 450 million has been allocated for 2027 for the construction of fully-fledged Faculties of Medicine and clinical buildings at the Universities of Peradeniya and Wayamba, with an investment of Rs. 8,000 million. • Rs. 2,250 million has been allocated for the development of academic and infrastructure facilities, including the construction of a fully-fledged building for the Faculty of Science of the Eastern University, laboratory complexes with computer and research facilities for the Universities of Ruhuna and Sabaragamuwa, buildings for the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences of the University of Jaffna and for the Faculty of Commerce and Management Studies

<!-- p. 24 -->

and the Faculty of Science of the University of Kelaniya, a laboratory complex for the Rajarata University, and multipurpose buildings for the Faculties of Management of the Uva Wellassa University and the University of Colombo. • Rs. 6,250 million has been allocated for ongoing student welfare work, including Rs. 717 million for development programmes such as improving the existing hostel facilities at the Hardy Advanced Technological Institute and the Universities of Moratuwa and Sri Jayewardenepura, providing clean drinking water and sanitation facilities for the Kilinochchi premises of the University of Jaffna, and developing wastewater treatment systems at the Universities of Sri Jayewardenepura, Eastern and Jaffna.

• It is proposed to commence a Rs. 2,050 million official quarters project in 2027 to provide residential facilities for the academic and non-academic staff of the University of Vavuniya. • Rs. 6,150 million has been estimated for the restoration of the damage caused to the infrastructure of the University of Peradeniya by Cyclone "Ditwah". In 2027, Rs. 700 million has been allocated for restoration work in higher educational institutions, including this.

• Although approximately 42,000 students are admitted to government universities annually, many students who qualify for university entry are denied this opportunity due to limited resources. With the objective of providing higher education opportunities for these students, the government has implemented a programme, facilitated through state banks, that offers interest-free loans to eligible students, enabling them to pursue degree programs at private higher education institutions. It is proposed to increase the monthly subsistence allowance currently available to these students from Rs.

6,250 to Rs. 10,000, effective from April 2027.

## 8.3 Vocational Education

Vocational education is being carried out at various levels through various departments and institutions, in more than 550 centres scattered across the country. Rs. 50 million has been allocated to conduct a study on centralizing direction and regulation within a single institution, with the aim of providing quality vocational education and the necessary infrastructure in them, producing the targeted labour force required by the country, and utilizing resources productively.

<!-- p. 25 -->

• In 2027, Rs. 800 million has been allocated for the Rs. 3,380 million project to develop hostel, canteen, laboratory and workshop facilities at the University of Vocational Technology in Ratmalana. • Rs. 200 million has been allocated in 2027 for improving the infrastructure of institutions belonging to the technical education and training sector, including the German Technical Training Institute and the Automobile Engineering Training Institute.

• Rs. 1,500 million has been allocated for the year 2027 for the project costing Rs. 6,000 million to modernize 25 selected Technical Colleges in 25 districts. It is expected to make a capital investment of Rs. 11 billion for the development of the vocational education and training sector, to modernize infrastructure, update curricula, integrate new technologies and strengthen collaboration between the public and private sectors.
