# 30. National Security

<!-- p. 63 -->

• Provisions of Rs. 250 million have been allocated for the establishment of a Defence Development Research Centre, affiliated to the Ministry of Defence, for research carried out by the security forces. • There are teams in the security forces recruited for construction work. It is proposed to provide a special allowance to these personnel when they are deployed in various development programmes of the Government.

• As a tribute to the parents of war heroes who joined the Armed Forces before 2009, it is proposed that the Ministry of Defence expeditiously prepare a programme to increase the monthly allowance of Rs. 750 currently paid to the mother and the father, and to implement it from July 2027.

• An institution to provide consultancy services for public sector construction will be established by attaching professionals, including engineers, serving in the tri-forces. This institution will function as a consultancy institution in the planning, monitoring and supervision of Government projects.

• Steps will be taken to modernize the digital and cyber security systems of the Ministry of Defence, establish a digital forensic laboratory and develop border security technological facilities. It is also expected to improve the communication and technological facilities of the Sri Lanka Army, and to provide new patrol craft, tug boats and modern radar systems to strengthen the maritime security and surveillance capabilities of the Sri Lanka Navy.
