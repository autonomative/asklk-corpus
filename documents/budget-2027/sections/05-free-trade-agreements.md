# 5. Free Trade Agreements

<!-- p. 15 -->

We expect to update the existing Free Trade Agreements and enter into new trade agreements with more countries in order to connect Sri Lanka to the global trade network. • An officials' committee has already been appointed by the Cabinet of Ministers to expand the existing Free Trade Agreement with India.

• Attention has been paid to a new Free Trade Agreement with China. • An application has been made to accede to the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP). • It is expected to enter into Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements with the United Arab Emirates and Oman.

• The "Sri Lanka–European Union Sustainable Trade and Economic Integration Project" will be implemented under a European Union grant of Rs. 3,430 million during the period 2027-2030, to promote export competitiveness, export diversification and sustainable production.

Further, we will work to enter into new Free Trade Agreements with other countries following a feasibility study.
