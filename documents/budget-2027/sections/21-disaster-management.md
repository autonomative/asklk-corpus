# 21. Disaster Management

<!-- p. 47 -->

• The installation of a modern Doppler Radar System for highly accurate weather forecasting, received as a JICA grant, will commence in 2027. • Rs. 1,000 million has been allocated for the rehabilitation of safe centres to ensure the safety of people during disasters • Rs. 500 million has been allocated to provide disaster response equipment essential for strengthening early warning.

• Rs. 670 million has been allocated to establish an island-wide digital radio communication system. • Rs. 500 million has been allocated for the rehabilitation of 77 existing coastal early warning towers, the construction of 120 new land-based towers covering

<!-- p. 48 -->

flood, landslide and tsunami-prone areas in 25 districts, and the establishment of a multi-hazard early warning communication system. Including the above proposals, Rs. 2,900 million has been allocated for 13 new disaster management projects. • A Dam Safety Act will be introduced to provide the legal safeguards necessary to ensure the safety of major dam systems.
