# 10. Development of Expressways and Highways

<!-- p. 26 -->

Through this Budget, we expect to make the largest investment in the development of highways, expressways and railways. Rs. 251 billion has been allocated for this purpose in this Budget. Further, we expect to spend Rs. 487 billion, including Rs. 146 billion as new proposals and Rs. 90 billion from the Ditwah Supplementary Estimate for the restoration of damage caused to railways, highways and bridges by Cyclone Ditwah. Mainly;

• It is proposed to allocate Rs. 30,000 million for the construction from Kurunegala to Galewela as the first step of the Kurunegala–Dambulla expressway section, which is the 4th phase of the Central Expressway. • An amount of Rs. 18,680 million has been allocated in 2026 for the initial activities of the development of the first phase of the Ruwanpura Expressway from Kahathuduwa to Ingiriya, and it is proposed to allocate Rs. 27,000 million in 2027.

<!-- p. 27 -->

• Rs. 53,450 million has been allocated for the year 2027 with the aim of completing the Kadawatha–Mirigama section of the Central Expressway by September 2028, which had been stalled due to the collapse of the economy. • Rs. 4,500 million has been allocated for the year 2027 with the aim of completing the Pothuhera–Rambukkana section of the Central Expressway by May 2027.

• Rs. 23,050 million has been allocated for the year 2027 with the aim of completing the Rambukkana–Galagedara section of the Central Expressway, which commenced in 2026 by July 2029. • In order to obtain maximum benefit from the Central Expressway, it is essential to improve the identified connecting road sections associated with it. It is proposed to allocate Rs. 500 million for the development of connecting roads associated with the Kurunegala–Dambulla Expressway, and Rs. 5,000 million and Rs. 3,000 million, respectively, for the development and rehabilitation of connecting roads associated with the Pothuhera–Galagedara and Kadawatha– Mirigama sections of the Central Expressway.

• It is also proposed to construct several new flyovers to minimize traffic congestion and improve transport efficiency. Accordingly, Rs. 1,900 million has been allocated in this Budget for the construction of 2 flyovers at the Alawwa and Kurunegala–Muttettugala railway crossings, and a new flyover near the Kandy Post Office to provide access to the newly constructed Kandy Multimodal Transport Hub bypassing the Kandy–Matale railway line.

• Rs. 600 million has been allocated for the construction of the Kokkilai Bridge, Rs. 400 million for the construction of the Kiran Bridge, Rs. 200 million for the construction of the Ponduhalchenai Bridge, Rs. 340 million for the construction of a new bridge across the Verugal River in the Punnaiyadi area, and Rs. 500 million for the construction of the Vadduvakkal Bridge in 2027. The total estimate for these projects is Rs. 10,630 million.

• Rs. 22,000 million has been allocated for the widening and development of selected road sections in order to minimize traffic congestion in the road network and ensure road safety. • Rs. 680 million has been allocated for carrying out feasibility studies on road developments identified from time to time, including the Ratnapura Bypass,
