# 15. Industry, Trade and Investment

<!-- p. 34 -->

• Rs. 4,000 million has been allocated for the development of the Valachchenai Industrial Estate, carrying out feasibility studies and preparing preliminary plans for the Puliyankulam Industrial Estate, completing further development work on new industrial estates including Ingiriya, Dambulla, Raigama– Millaniya and Katunayake, and improving infrastructure facilities in existing industrial estates.

• Rs. 750 million has been allocated for activities including holding "Sri Lanka Expo 2027" in January, and promoting export diversification, competitiveness and high-value exports by implementing the priorities identified under the National Export Development Plan (NEDP).

• A feasibility study will be carried out to establish a knowledge and experience centre that expands links between industry and universities, promotes research and development, and supports the commercialization of new products and processes. • The Kurunchantivu Salt Pan, where production activities had been halted until now, will be restarted using the funds of the National Salt Limited with the aim of creating employment opportunities in the Northern region. The General Treasury has provided Rs. 236 million for the year 2026 for the construction of the necessary access roads and the provision of services for this purpose.

• The National Gem and Jewellery Authority Act, No. 50 of 1993 will be amended to include the necessary legal provisions for issuing licences to the jewellery industry. • The acquisition of the land required for the establishment of an International Gem and Jewellery Trade Hub, where gem and jewellery trade, export facilities, laboratory services and licensing services can be obtained from a single location, is being carried out, and a preliminary feasibility study is expected to be carried out by the University of Moratuwa.

• Rs. 1,860 million has been allocated for the construction of the Narahenpita building to relocate the Ministry of Industries, presently located in Kollupitiya, together with all institutions under the Ministry, to a single location.

<!-- p. 35 -->

Encouraging Investment through the Capital Market • At present, the number of persons actively trading in connection with the Colombo Stock Exchange is only around 0.7 percent of the population over 18 years of age. Therefore, it is proposed to implement a new programme named "A Share for Everyone" under the vision "Let's Know, Let's Learn, Let's Invest".

With the aim of actively connecting students in schools who are studying for the Advanced Level in Grade 13 and are over 18 years of age with the stock market, it is proposed to introduce the project to provide a share portfolio subject to a maximum of Rs. 10,000/-, in accordance with a formal selection procedure.

• It is proposed to introduce a mechanism to encourage the listing on the stock market of small and medium-scale enterprises (SME Listing Support Mechanism), which makes a significant contribution to the economy and job creation. • For the above proposals, the Colombo Stock Exchange will deploy Rs. 250 million, and it is proposed to allocate an additional Rs. 500 million for this purpose.

Mineral Resources We have still failed to properly integrate the mineral resources available in the country into our economy. There is great potential in our country to promote an industrial system associated with mineral resources. The steps required to study the potential of these mineral resources and to integrate the benefits derived from mineral resources into the domestic economy have been presented in the National Policy on Mineral Resources. The Geological Survey and Mines Bureau will take the necessary steps to carry out preliminary feasibility studies of mineral resources, and steps will be taken to obtain specialized technical knowledge internationally for the exploration of mineral deposits.

• Exploration of the quantity of the graphite reserve in the Kahatagaha Graphite Mine, which extends over an area of 102 acres, is scheduled to be carried out jointly with the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau. Once the size of the reserve has been identified, further steps are scheduled to be taken to identify a suitable investor for the graphite value addition project, including graphite mining and processing activities.

<!-- p. 36 -->

• Cabinet approval has been granted to allocate 58 grids containing mineral sands along the eastern coastal belt to Lanka Mineral Sands Limited. A Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee and a Project Committee have been appointed by the Cabinet of Ministers to select an investor for a value addition industry for the mineral sands mined by Lanka Mineral Sands Limited.

• Exploration is already being carried out on the silica sand deposit extending over an area of 40 acres in the Nattandiya area. After identifying the potential of the relevant mineral resources, steps will be taken to identify a suitable investor in 2027. • An area of 28 metric grid units has been identified in relation to the Kotigambokka quartz deposit located in the Wellawaya area. Steps will be taken to identify a suitable investor in 2027 for the mining and value addition project of this quartz deposit.
