2.2 Removal of Exemption VAT exemption applicable on following items will be removed. HS Code Description 0511.91.20 Artemia eggs 1404.90.93 Coconut coir pith 2.3 Revision of the basis of calculation of VAT on Film Tickets The VAT liability of film producers, distributors, the National Film Corporation and cinema hall operators will be determined based on the respective share of cinema ticket revenue received or appropriated by each party. The basis for calculating VAT liability will be revised to apply ticket value after Entertainment Tax but before VAT.

2.4 Amendment of the Definition of “Eligible Exporter” The definition of the term “eligible exporter” will be amended to recognize the Newly VAT registered persons as eligible exporters where, more than 80% of their turnover during the preceding 06 months has been derived from exports.

2.5 Specifying Exchange Rate for VAT on Supplies made for Foreign Currency The indicative exchange rate published by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, as may be specified by the CGIR will be applied in converting foreign currency into Sri Lankan rupees for VAT purposes.

2.6 Validity of Electronic Tax Invoices The digital or electronic invoices will be recognized as legally valid similarly as paper-based invoices. The format, particulars, technical and procedural requirements of electronic invoice will be specified by the CGIR, by order published in the Gazette.

3. Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL) [Amendments to the Social Security Contribution Levy Act, No.25 of 2022] Exemption on the Supply of Bunker Fuel to Ships Amendments will be introduced to expressly exempt the bunker fuel supplied to ships engaged in international transportation from SSCL.

4. Simplification of the Tax Structure Applicable to the Betting and Gaming Industry Amendments will be introduced to Betting and Gaming Levy Act, No. 40 of 1988 and other relevant tax laws to establish a simplified, transparent and coherent tax structure for the betting and gaming industry, which will be easy to administer and comply with.

5. Revision of Special Commodity Levy (SCL) SCL applicable on the import of certain essential food Items will be revised with effect from November 13, 2026, as follows: - SCL will be reduced to Rs. 10 per Kilogram for sprats and Maldive fish; - SCL will be reduced by Rs. 50 per Kilogram for powdered and unpowdered dried chilies;

- SCL will be increased by Rs. 10 per Kilogram for sugar. 6. Phased Reduction of PARA-Tariffs Following revisions will be made as a part of the phased removal of Para- Tariffs: 6.1 Removal of Ports and Airports Development levy (PAL) Effective from January 1, 2027, the Port and Airport Development Levy will be removed on the importation of newsprint and batteries used for solar energy storage.

6.2 CESS

CESS rates will be reduced by 25% across 800 HS Code lines. 7. Strengthening Tax Administration and Facilitating Taxpayer Compliance 7.1 Certificate of an approved accountant or an authorized person as may be specified will be accepted by the CGIR to verify the taxpayer compliance with existing statutory restriction on the cash transactions.

7.2 Anti-avoidance rules will be introduced to counter the potential tax avoidance practices, in company amalgamations /mergers, while ensuring that the genuine amalgamations are not unduly affected. 7.3 Provisions will be introduced to prevent double taxation of donations between NGOs subject to the submission of proof to confirm the tax was paid by the original recipient NGO.

7.4 Section 73 of the Inland Revenue Act, No. 24 of 2017 (IRA) will be amended to determine the source of income derived from remote and digital services, including online advertising and cloud services, by reference to the place of payment, thereby ensuring clarity in their income tax treatment.

7.5 Section 75 (5) of the IRA will be amended by replacing the expression “the prevention of fiscal evasion” with “the prevention of tax evasion and avoidance”, to align with the terminology used in the OECD and UN Model Double Taxation Conventions. 7.6 Section 85(1B) of the IRA will be amended, to clarify that the 14% withholding tax on service fees and insurance premiums applies only to payments made to non-resident persons who do not have a permanent establishment in Sri Lanka.

7.7 Input tax claims previously disallowed due to delays in submitting VAT returns during the COVID-19 pandemic will be allowed to claim by the eligible taxpayers by recognizing the outstanding returns for that period are filed on or before July 31, 2026, as validly filed

7.8 Confidentiality provisions of the IRA will be amended to ensure that information received from foreign tax authorities is disclosed only to persons authorised under the Act and with the prior consent of the relevant foreign tax authority. 7.9 Necessary amendments will be introduced to the VAT Act to:- - Allow uniform periods of 12 months for local purchases and 24 months for imports to claim input tax, irrespective of the VAT return submission date.

- Rectify discrepancies between the Sinhala and English texts of the relevant provisions relating to input tax claims. - Align the issuance of credit and debit notes with the time of input tax claims and provide for tax adjustments where tax invoices have not been issued.

- Clarify the VAT treatment of transactions conducted on a Non-Foreign Exchange (NFE) basis, transactions involving non-tax invoices, the consequences arising from VAT exemptions and other related matters. 8. Abolition of Tourism Development Levy (TDL) Tourism Development Levy (TDL) imposed under the Finance Act, No.25 of 2003, will be abolished with effect from April 01, 2027.

9. Effective date of Amendments Unless specifically mentioned otherwise in this Technical Note or the relevant Tax Legislation, amendments proposed herein before will be effective from April 1, 2027. 10.
