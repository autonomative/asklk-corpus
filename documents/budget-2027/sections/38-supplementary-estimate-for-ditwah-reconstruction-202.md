# 38. Supplementary Estimate for Ditwah Reconstruction – 2027

<!-- p. 79 -->

It is proposed to allocate as provisions for 2027 the amount that has not been utilized so far, due to reasons including the war situation in the Middle East, out of the Supplementary Estimate of Rs. 500 billion approved by Parliament for the reconstruction of Ditwah damage. Budgetary provisions of Rs. 260 billion have to be secured again for 2027 to properly complete the Ditwah reconstruction activities.

Accordingly, in addition to the 2027 Budget I have presented, it is proposed to present to Parliament a Supplementary Estimate for Ditwah reconstruction expenditure in accordance with the provisions of Section 16 of the Public Financial Management Act. It is proposed to table that Supplementary Estimate today, following the Budget Speech.
