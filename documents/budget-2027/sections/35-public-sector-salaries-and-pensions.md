# 35. Public Sector Salaries and Pensions

<!-- p. 68 -->

**Honourable Speaker,**

Through our first Budget in 2025, steps were taken to increase the basic salary by increasing the minimum monthly basic salary of the public sector by Rs. 15,750 and by increasing the value of the annual salary increment by 80 percent. Rs. 330 billion is spent on this salary increase. This salary revision has been implemented in 3 phases, and its final phase will be implemented in 2027, for which Rs. 110 billion is allocated in the Budget.

Meanwhile, public servants in various sectors have submitted requests to revise their allowances, considering the increase in the related expenses they have to bear in the performance of their duties. Taking into account those requests as well as the fiscal space currently available to the Government, we expect to increase several allowances on a priority basis through this Budget. Requests to revise other allowances will be considered in phases in future Budgets, taking into account the fiscal capacity of the Government.

Accordingly, it is proposed to revise allowances as follows. Transport Allowance of Executive Officers in the Public Service • Executive officers who are entitled to an official vehicle under Public Administration Circular No. 05/2016 but use their private vehicles in the performance of official duties are currently paid a monthly transport allowance of Rs. 50,000. Considering the increase in maintenance, repair and other operational costs when private vehicles are used for official duties, and since it is more cost-effective for the Government for officers who own a vehicle to use their private vehicle rather than being provided with a Government vehicle, in order to encourage this, it is proposed to increase the monthly transport allowance currently paid to Rs. 100,000.

<!-- p. 69 -->

• With the aim of encouraging the use of electric vehicles, it is proposed to increase by 150 percent, with effect from July 2027, the monthly allowance paid in terms of Public Administration Circular 13/2008(V) to officers entitled to an official vehicle who use fully electric private vehicles.

Allowances in the Health Sector • Considering the important role they play in safeguarding the health and well- being of our people, it is proposed to increase the DAT allowance paid to medical officers by Rs. 25,000, from Rs. 70,000 to Rs. 95,000. • To strengthen the national health system, it is proposed to increase the monthly special duty allowance of nursing officers and officers in the paramedical and supplementary medical services from Rs. 6,000 to Rs. 12,000, and the monthly special duty allowance of other health staff from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,000.

• It is proposed to pay the above allowance increases proposed for the health sector in two steps. Accordingly, 50 percent of the increase will be paid from July 2027, and the remaining 50 percent in 2028. Payments to Lecturers in Universities and Vocational Training Institutes • Considering the important role played by university academic staff in improving the quality of higher education, promoting research activities and developing human resources, it is proposed to increase the monthly academic allowance currently paid to such staff by Rs. 25,000. It is expected to pay 50 percent of this increase from July 2027, and the remaining 50 percent from July 2028.

• In order to attract external lecturers with specialist knowledge to universities for academic activities, it is proposed to increase the payments made to external lecturers threefold in line with the existing rates. Accordingly, it is proposed to increase the allowance currently paid per hour from Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 4,500. Further, it is proposed to increase the allowance of Rs. 600 paid per hour to external advisers for practical examinations to Rs. 1,800.

• In order to attract external lecturers with specialist knowledge to vocational and technical institutions for vocational skills education, it is proposed to increase the payments made to external lecturers by 100 percent in line with the existing rates.

<!-- p. 70 -->

Food Allowance of the Tri-Forces • Considering the increase in the cost incurred for the food of members of the tri- forces, and with the aim of meeting their nutritional requirements and ensuring their welfare, it is proposed to increase the daily food allowance paid to the tri-forces, with effect from July 2027, by Rs. 315 for officers and by Rs.

332 for other ranks. Food and Accommodation Allowance of Officers of the Sri Lanka Police • Considering the increase in food and accommodation expenses that police officers have to bear in the performance of their duties, it is proposed to increase the monthly food and accommodation allowance currently paid to meet such expenses by Rs. 10,000 with effect from July 2027.

Risk Allowance for Prison Officers • Taking into account the difficult and high-risk working conditions faced by prison officers in the performance of their duties, steps will be taken to increase the monthly risk allowance currently paid by 100 percent, from Rs. 8,000 to Rs.

16,000. • Likewise, it is proposed to increase the allowance of Rs. 2,250 currently paid to civil officers serving in the Department of Prisons to Rs. 10,000. • It is proposed to pay these proposed allowances of officers serving in the Department of Prisons with effect from July 2027.

Allowances for Field Officers • Considering the role played by field officers in directly providing essential public services to the community, and the increase in the costs associated with their field-based duties, it is proposed to increase the monthly travelling allowance currently paid to Grama Niladharis from Rs. 1,600 to Rs. 4,000, and the monthly office rent allowance from Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 6,000 for Municipal Council and Urban Council areas and from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,000 for Pradeshiya Sabha areas.

• The monthly travelling allowance currently paid to Development Officers will be increased from Rs. 300 to Rs. 1,000, and the monthly office rent allowance from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 2,000 for Municipal Council and Urban Council areas and from Rs. 750 to Rs. 1,500 for Pradeshiya Sabha areas.

<!-- p. 71 -->

• The monthly travelling allowance currently paid to Samurdhi Officers will be increased from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 2,500, and the monthly office rent allowance from Rs. 2,500 to Rs. 5,000 for Municipal Council and Urban Council areas and from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,000 for Pradeshiya Sabha areas.

• It is proposed to increase the monthly travelling allowance currently paid to Agriculture Research and Production Assistants from Rs. 400 to Rs. 1,000, and the monthly office rent allowance from Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 6,000 for Municipal Council and Urban Council areas and from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,000 for Pradeshiya Sabha areas.

• The above allowances for field officers will be revised from April 2027. Revision of Allowances in the Judicial Sector Allowances of High Court Judges and Judicial Officers • Considering the professional responsibilities, the nature of service and the role of officers in the judicial sector, it is proposed to increase by Rs. 25,000 the monthly professional allowance currently paid to High Court Judges, Special Grade Judicial Officers and Judicial Officers of other grades. Steps will be taken to pay 50 percent of this increase from July 2027, and the remaining 50 percent in 2028.

• With effect from July 2027, it is proposed to increase by Rs. 50,000 the monthly transport allowance currently paid to officers entitled to official vehicles, to increase the monthly driver allowance paid to entitled officers from Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 50,000, and, considering the increase in the expenses that have to be borne to meet their residential needs in instances where the Government has not provided an official residence, whether owned or obtained on a lease basis, to increase by 100 percent the monthly housing allowance currently paid to High Court Judges and other Judges.

Allowances of the Attorney General's Department • It is proposed to increase the monthly non-practising allowance by Rs. 25,000 for the posts of Attorney General, Solicitor General and Senior State Counsel, and by Rs. 20,000 for officers in the posts of Senior State Attorney and State Attorney of the Attorney General's Department. Steps will be taken to pay 50 percent of this increase from July 2027, and the remaining 50 percent in 2028.

<!-- p. 72 -->

• With effect from July 2027, it is proposed to increase the monthly transport allowance paid to officers entitled to official vehicles by Rs. 50,000, the monthly driver allowance paid to entitled officers from Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 50,000, and the housing allowance currently paid by 100 percent.

Allowances of the Legal Draftsman's Department • It is proposed to increase the special allowance of officers in the posts of Legal Draftsman, Additional Legal Draftsman and Deputy Legal Draftsman of the Legal Draftsman's Department by Rs. 25,000, and the special allowance of Senior Assistant Legal Draftsmen and Assistant Legal Draftsmen by Rs. 20,000.

Steps will be taken to pay 50 percent of this increase from July 2027, and the remaining 50 percent in 2028. • With effect from July 2027, the monthly driver allowance paid to entitled officers will be increased from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 50,000. Allowances of Judicial Support Staff • Taking into account the role played by judicial support staff in maintaining the efficient functioning of the judicial system, their workload and the responsibilities assigned to them, steps will also be taken to increase the relevant allowances paid to these officers with effect from April 2027.

Monthly Allowances of Independent Commissions, Other Commissions and Statutory Institutions • As the monthly allowances of the Chairpersons and members of Independent Commissions and other Commissions have not been revised since 2018, and the monthly allowances of the Chairpersons, Executive Directors and Working Directors of State Corporations, Statutory Institutions and Government-owned Companies have not been increased since 2015, it is proposed to increase their monthly allowances with effect from July 2027, taking into account the responsibilities they hold.

• The monthly allowances paid to the Chairpersons and members of other Commissions will be revised as per the Annexure VI.

Revision of Public Service Pensions

**Honourable Speaker,**

Pension Revision and Relief to Pensioners • The Government is implementing a phased pension revision programme with the objective of ensuring a fair and structured pension system for all pensioners by systematically eliminating the disparities that exist among the pensions of retired public servants.

• Through the first Budget in 2025, steps were taken to convert, in three phases, the pensions of public servants who retired prior to 2020 to the 2020 basic salary scale, and its first and second phases have already been completed. Its final phase is scheduled to be implemented in 2027.

• It is also the objective of the Government to minimize and eliminate in phases, taking into account sustainable public financial management, the disparity that has arisen, due to the new basic salary revision implemented in 2025, between the pensions of public servants who retired before 2025 and those retiring in or after 2025.

• It is proposed to provide a monthly interim allowance of Rs. 2,500, with effect from July 01, 2027, as a relief to pensioners who retired during the period from 2020 to 2024, until the pension revision scheduled to be carried out under the new 2025 basic salary structure is implemented.

• The forthcoming pension revision based on the new 2025 basic salary structure is planned to commence with the 2028 Budget and to be implemented in several phases. Once this revision is applied to the relevant pensioners and the payment of the revised pension commences, the payment of the above- mentioned monthly interim allowance of Rs. 2,500 proposed for those who retired during the period 2020–2024 will cease.

• These provisions will also apply to public sector pensioners whose salaries were determined under Department of Management Services Circulars 02/2025, 03/2025, 04/2025 and 05/2025. • The overall objective of all these measures is to provide fair relief to public servants who retired at different times by systematically narrowing the pension disparities among them, and to establish a sustainable pension system in the long term.
