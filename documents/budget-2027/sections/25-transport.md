# 25. Transport

<!-- p. 54 -->

It is the expectation of our Government to usher in a new era in the transport sector to ensure an efficient, comfortable public transport service. Accordingly, a record Rs. 95,000 million will be deployed for modernizing railway and passenger bus services, strengthening connectivity between transport networks and promoting sustainable public transport.

• It is expected to extend the Metro Bus service introduced around Colombo this year to the urban areas of Kandy, Galle and Jaffna as well. • The Government has invested in Metro Buses up to now. More than 120 buses are already in operation. A further 50 buses are scheduled to be added to that company in 2027. In addition, we have also planned to introduce electric buses.

100 buses are scheduled to be received for this purpose as a donation from the Government of China. Activities such as the construction of the depots required for these buses, the provision of the necessary charging facilities and the provision of training will commence next year, and provisions of Rs. 4,500 million have been allocated for this purpose.

<!-- p. 55 -->

• To encourage the private sector to deploy Metro Buses as well, existing route permit holders will be encouraged to add a modern low-floor bus to operation in place of their existing buses. • Steps have already been taken to import 600 air-conditioned buses to provide a comfortable and safe service to long-distance passengers.

• It is proposed to allocate Rs. 1,000 million to purchase 50 small buses of 22 seats required for rural areas with narrow hill roads. • It is proposed to allocate Rs. 4,000 million to obtain 200 small buses of 33 seats required for passenger services in rural areas.

• Rs. 200 million has been allocated for the Digital Transformation and Integrated Management Systems Development Project of the Sri Lanka Transport Board. • It is proposed to allocate Rs. 600 million to establish the Department of Motor Traffic on the Werahera premises in order to provide a more efficient service.

• Rs. 700 million has been allocated to establish a modern digital system for the process of vehicle registration and transfer of ownership, with the aim of providing efficient and transparent services to the public. • Rs. 1,500 million has been allocated to establish a new digital Driver Demerit Points System and a road safety enforcement system, integrating the Department of Motor Traffic and the Sri Lanka Police, to minimize road accidents and for road safety.

• Rs. 5,000 million has been allocated to purchase 5 new trains for the railway service. • Provisions of Rs. 2,000 million have been allocated in 2027 for the programme to repair railway stations that have not been properly maintained for a long time. • Train operations between Colombo and Peradeniya have been resumed by deploying the resources of the Department of Railways with maximum efficiency to restore the Up-Country railway line damaged by the Ditwah disaster.

• Under the Government of India loan assistance programme, the work required to rehabilitate the railway section from Rambukkana to Peradeniya, the Up-

Country railway line from Peradeniya to Gampola, and the Matale railway line in a safer and more stable manner, in accordance with modern engineering standards, has commenced. It is expected that trains will be able to operate up to Kandy within the first quarter of 2027.

Accordingly, it is expected to fully rehabilitate the Up-Country railway line and restore train operations up to Badulla by the middle of 2027. • The Government of India provided assistance for the rehabilitation of infrastructure damaged by Cyclone Ditwah, the preparation of housing and water supply projects, the rehabilitation of 13 bridges including the Moragahakanda Bridge, the construction of Bailey bridges, and to sectors including health and education.

• Under the Cyclone Ditwah damage rehabilitation programme of the Government of China, rehabilitation work on 9 bridges located in the Gampaha, Kegalle, Ampara, Kurunegala, Polonnaruwa and Batticaloa districts has been prepared through a grant for the reconstruction of 14 destroyed bridges.
