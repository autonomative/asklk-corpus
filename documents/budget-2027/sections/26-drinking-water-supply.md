# 26. Drinking Water Supply

<!-- p. 56 -->

Rs. 77,000 million has been allocated for the uninterrupted supply of clean drinking water to the people, the expansion of sanitation facilities, and the supply of adequate quantities of water for economic activities, including industries. Further, the invitation of private investment is being considered.

• Rs. 5,190 million has been allocated to commence 13 new projects, including in Jaffna, Mannar, Mullaitivu, Vavuniya, Nagadeepa, Valachchenai and Kotte, to meet drinking water requirements. • It is proposed to allocate Rs. 1,800 million for the year 2027 for the commencement of the Kundasale–Haragama Water Supply Project, with a cost of Rs. 34,368 million.

• It is proposed to allocate Rs. 1,000 million for the commencement of the Eheliyagoda Water Supply Project, with a cost of Rs. 4,775 million. • It is proposed to allocate Rs. 1,800 million for the commencement of the Greater Kilinochchi Water Supply Project, with a cost of Rs. 41,140 million, covering

<!-- p. 57 -->

the Karachchi, Kandavalai, Poonakary and Pachchilaipalli Divisional Secretariat Divisions in the Kilinochchi district. • Rs. 3,000 million has been allocated for the commencement of the Greater Galle City Water Supply Project, with a cost of Rs. 37,501 million.

• Rs. 700 million has been allocated for the commencement of the Haldummulla– Beragala Water Supply Project, with a cost of Rs. 3,262 million. • Rs. 300 million has been allocated for the commencement in 2027 of the Yatimahana–Ma Oya Water Supply Project, with a cost of Rs. 22,158 million.

• Provisions will continuously be provided to resolve step by step the problems relating to drinking water in the Jaffna and Vanni districts, such as seawater intrusion, high hardness, high mineral salts and bacterial contamination. It is proposed to allocate Rs. 1,000 million for this purpose.

• Rs. 12,000 million has been allocated to implement projects, including improving the capacity of existing drinking water projects, expanding distribution and constructing sanitation facilities. • Rs. 250 million has been allocated for feasibility studies to meet the water requirements for industrial and commercial activities in the areas of Kuliyapitiya, Pannala, Bingiriya, Udubaddawa, Trincomalee and Gampola– Jayamalapura.

• Rs. 2,000 million has been allocated for improving community water supply schemes across the island. • Rs. 1,740 million has been allocated to implement the Ella, Moratuwa and Ekala wastewater management projects with the aim of improving sanitation facilities and managing wastewater in a formal manner.
