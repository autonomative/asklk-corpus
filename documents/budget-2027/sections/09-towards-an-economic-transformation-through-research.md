# 9. Towards an Economic Transformation through Research, Development and Innovation

<!-- p. 25 -->

**Honourable Speaker,**

The National Council for Research and Development has been established for policy guidance in the field of science and technology and for directing and coordinating research activities. Through this, its main function is to identify all national research (science, technology, engineering, agriculture, medicine) and to work by giving priority to research that contributes to the production economy.

This will not be limited to science and technology activities, but will also undertake research relating to defence and social welfare activities. Through this, • Monitoring and regulating research and development activities carried out by the public as well as the private sector.

• Directing research in a manner that contributes to national development goals and economic growth. • Developing the capabilities of local researchers and supporting advanced research of international standard. • Transforming the results of research carried out in laboratories and universities into products or services useful to society and the industrial sector.

<!-- p. 26 -->

• Preparing national research and development policies to suit the future needs of the country. • Taking new technologies discovered through research to commercialization and integrating them into the country's economy. The National Innovation Agency, Sri Lanka Inventors Commission, the National Science Foundation and the National Research Council, the National Science and Technology Commission, and the Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy, which come under the Ministry of Science and Technology, are scheduled to be incorporated into this National Council for Research and Development.

Steps will also be taken to provide the institutional, technical and infrastructure facilities necessary to carry out research covering various subject areas through research institutions and other public institutions under various Ministries. Through Government investment, Rs. 23,800 million has been allocated for research and development programmes in 2027.

Rs. 7,600 million, including operational expenses, has been allocated to coordinate all such research activities under the Ministry of Science and Technology. The National Research and Development Bill has been drafted. It is expected to be presented to this Parliament in the first half of 2027.
