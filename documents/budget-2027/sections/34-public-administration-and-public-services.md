# 34. Public Administration and Public Services

<!-- p. 66 -->

• A four-year project will be implemented to enhance the revenue-generating capacity of Local Government Institutions, utilize existing resources productively and strengthen financial sustainability. Through this, it is expected to continuously maintain the essential services of Local Government Institutions, and provisions of Rs. 1,000 million have been allocated for this purpose.

• It is proposed to provide provisions of Rs. 575 million from Provincial Council funds to complete the construction of the Capital Square building, which was commenced by the Kurunegala Municipal Council and abandoned without being completed. • It is proposed to allocate provisions of Rs. 50,000 million, at Rs. 2,000 million per district, to implement the District Development Programme and the Decentralized Budget Programme. Of this, Rs. 47,750 million will be allocated for special development programmes identified at district level, and the remaining Rs. 2,250 million for implementing the Decentralized Budget Programme, at Rs. 10 million per Member of Parliament.

• Plans are in place to improve both roads and bridges as part of the development of the rural road network. A sum of Rs. 55,000 million has been allocated for this purpose. In addition, Rs. 5,500 million has been allocated for the improvement of rural bridges including those under the purview of local government authorities while Rs. 20,000 million has been set aside for the maintenance and rehabilitation of Provincial Council roads. Accordingly, when the allocations under the District Development Programme are added to the aforementioned Rs. 80,000 million, a total of approximately Rs. 100,000 million has been earmarked for the development of rural roads.

<!-- p. 67 -->

• Rs. 1,100 million has been allocated for the construction of new District Secretariat offices in the Vavuniya, Puttalam and Anuradhapura districts with the aim of providing an efficient and quality public service to the public. • Rs. 265 million has been allocated for the commencement of the construction of new Divisional Secretariat office buildings in Dehiattakandiya and Elapatha to further formalize public services at the regional level.

• It is proposed to introduce a fairer and simpler mechanism, effective from the year 2027, to address the difficulties arising from the current system which limits the allocation of funds for salaries in local government institutions to 20% and 40%. Accordingly, it is proposed to cover 100% of the funding required for staff salary expenditures in 46 financially weak local government authorities in 2027. It is also proposed to change these percentages for the other local government institutions. Furthermore, Rs. 1,750 million has been allocated to support capital programmes, based on plans submitted by these institutions.

• Provisions of Rs. 1,100 million have been allocated to complete the construction of the Kegalle and Nuwara Eliya District Secretariat offices and the Negombo, Homagama, Kalutara, Pitabeddara, Angunakolapelessa and Deraniyagala Divisional Secretariat offices, work on which has already commenced, in order to enhance the efficiency of public officers and provide a quality service to the public.

• Rs. 4,500 million has been allocated to provide a new stock of computers in place of the old stock of computers held by public institutions. It is proposed to provide a further Rs. 2,200 million in addition. • It is proposed to resolve the issues existing within departmental services and other executive services, in alignment with the promotion mechanisms currently in place for island-wide services.

• In order to provide adequate human resources for maintaining an efficient and quality public service in public institutions, approval has already been granted by the officials' committee on staff management appointed by the Cabinet of Ministers and the Department of Management Services for recruitment to 148,940 vacancies, while reviewing the recruitment process of the public service. Of this number, around 40,000 recruitments have been completed so far, and provisions will be allocated to recruit further 110,000 in the future.

<!-- p. 68 -->

• The Sethsiripaya Stage III Project in Battaramulla has commenced. This project is expected to be completed in 2028, and Rs. 6,800 million has been allocated for this purpose. • It is proposed to implement the Sethsiripaya Stage IV Project, with a day care centre, at Denzil Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Battaramulla, by deploying the funds of the Urban Development Authority.
