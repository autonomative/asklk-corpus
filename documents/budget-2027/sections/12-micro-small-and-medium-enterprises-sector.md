# 12. Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Sector

<!-- p. 28 -->

We have recognized that the development of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) is most essential for a rapid transformation of the economy. Accordingly, in 2026, we have allocated Rs. 54,136 million for interest subsidies and refinancing for a number of schemes such as collateral-free loans, interest-free loans and low-interest loans. Through this, the State and private banking system has been able to grant loans amounting to Rs. 97,100 million owing to the surge that took place in that sector. Owing to the surge in that sector, we have been able to provide this entire allocation within this year.

Also, in 2027, Rs. 67,687 million has been allocated for interest subsidies and refinancing to banks. Through this, it is expected to provide Rs. 174 billion as concessionary loans to entrepreneurs through State and private banks. This includes the following loan schemes.

<!-- p. 29 -->

• Refinance and Interest Subsidy Loan Scheme for Reviving the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise Sector (Re-MSME Plus) o The Government will provide an interest subsidy for the granting of a further Rs. 20,000 million in loans, in addition to the Rs. 30,000 million currently allocated to provide concessionary loans at an interest rate of 5 percent for the capital and working capital requirements of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises under the Refinance Loan Scheme, and it is also proposed to allocate Rs. 200 million for this purpose as the first phase. Steps will be taken to provide the additional provisions required.

o In order to make the fisheries sector eligible for this concessionary loan scheme, it is proposed to grant loans to enterprises engaged in the fisheries industry by considering only the business turnover in the definition of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.

• Schemes Implemented with the Financial Assistance of the Asian Development Bank o In order to meet the existing demand for concessionary interest loan programmes, a further Rs. 30,000 million has been allocated, with the funding assistance of the Asian Development Bank, for granting loans for the capital and working capital requirements of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.

• Pledge Loan Scheme for Small and Medium-Scale Paddy Mill Owners o It is proposed to implement the Pledge Loan Scheme for small and medium- scale paddy mill owners for the purchase of paddy at the Government guaranteed price as an interest-free loan scheme from 2027. It is expected to provide financial facilities of Rs. 20,000 million through this programme. In addition to the Rs. 350 million currently allocated, further allocations are proposed to bear the additional interest subsidy of this loan scheme.

• Loan Scheme for Uplifting Women-headed Families o A special programme is essential for uplifting women-headed family units. In particular, the living standards of such families living in rural areas are at a relatively low level. In order to strengthen such families economically, it is proposed to implement a group loan scheme of up to Rs. 1 million at a concessionary annual interest rate of 3 percent through the Samurdhi Bank.

<!-- p. 30 -->

For this, it is proposed to allocate Rs. 200 million as the first phase for the payment of interest subsidies. Steps will be taken to provide the additional provisions required. Through this, it is expected to provide loans amounting to Rs. 12,500 million. • Programme for Empowering Rural Enterprises o With the aim of strengthening enterprises in rural areas and empowering Aswesuma beneficiaries, it is expected to provide loans to entrepreneurs through the Samurdhi Bank up to a maximum of Rs. 5 million at a concessionary annual interest rate of 4 percent. In order to qualify for this loan facility, it is mandatory for such entrepreneurs to provide employment opportunities to at least five Aswesuma beneficiaries. It is proposed to allocate Rs. 200 million for interest subsidies for this purpose. Through this, it is expected to provide loans amounting to Rs. 5,000 million.

• Concessionary Loans for Large-Scale Entrepreneurs in the Fisheries Industry o It is proposed to provide a maximum loan amount of up to Rs. 500 million through banks at an annual interest rate of 5 percent to large-scale fisheries industrialists with export potential, fish product suppliers, boat manufacturers and manufacturers of other fishing gear required for the fisheries industry, to obtain the modern technology and other inputs they require. It is proposed to provide Rs. 1,000 million as interest subsidies for this purpose. Through this, it is expected to provide loans amounting to Rs.

15,000 million. • Home-Stay Loan Scheme o It is proposed to provide concessionary loans of up to a maximum of Rs. 2 million at an annual interest rate of 3 percent to entrepreneurs for businesses operating home-based tourist accommodation, to improve such facilities and to start new home-based tourist accommodation, and it is proposed to allocate Rs. 100 million as the first phase for the payment of the necessary interest subsidies. Steps will be taken to provide the additional provisions required. It is expected to provide concessionary loans amounting to Rs. 7,500 million under this loan scheme.

<!-- p. 31 -->

The data and information required to access all these loan schemes have been included on the website of the Department of Development Finance of the Ministry of Finance, and a hotline of 1819 has also been introduced.
