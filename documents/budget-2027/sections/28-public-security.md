# 28. Public Security

<!-- p. 60 -->

• Rs. 9,500 million has been allocated for the digitalization of the duties of the Sri Lanka Police, the complete modernization of the National Police Academy, the development of the Police Hospital in Narahenpita, the development of the

<!-- p. 61 -->

Police Training School in Attidiya, the construction of the Police Sports Complex in Mirihana, and the upgrading of facilities and infrastructure at the Special Task Force camps in Gonahena, Kurunegala, Arantalawa, Kegalle and Kirindiwela. • It is proposed to allocate Rs. 200 million for feasibility studies and the preparation of plans to construct a new administrative building to house in a single building the various divisions of the Sri Lanka Police Department, which are currently operating in buildings obtained on a rental basis at various locations in the city of Colombo, with the aim of providing a more efficient service to the public.

• Rs. 500 million has been allocated for feasibility studies and the preparation of plans to construct a 15-storey building with the modern facilities required for investigating evolving crimes such as cybercrime and the suppression of financial fraud, for the Criminal Investigation Department, which is operating in a severely dilapidated building in Colombo Fort.

• It is proposed to provide police officers in June 2027 with a new uniform suited to the climatic conditions of our country and equipped with modern technological devices. • Rehabilitating persons addicted to dangerous drugs and reintegrating them into society is a long-term and important objective of the "Ratama Ekata" National Operation. For this, Rs. 180 million has been allocated for the development of infrastructure at the Senapura and Kandakadu rehabilitation centres and the full development of counselling and treatment services and vocational training facilities.

• Rs. 6,200 million has been allocated for activities including ensuring border security in line with national and international policies and standards on immigration and emigration, developing the Wattala Temporary Detention Centre with facilities conforming to international standards for foreigners temporarily detained due to visa crises and complications, establishing a new office complex in Trincomalee to enhance regional operations, and digitalizing immigration and emigration activities.

• Legislative work has already commenced to grant permanent residency to investors based on the size of the investment and other relevant criteria.
